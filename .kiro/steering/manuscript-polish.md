# Manuscript Polish

## Resurrect/result panels
- Light text on dark panels only (no dark-on-dark).
- Panel background: dark charcoal or deep burgundy.
- Text: off-white or pale cream (#f8f8f0 or similar).
- High contrast for accessibility.

## Manuscript leaf
- **Background:** CSS/SVG parchment texture (cream #f5e9d5, subtle grain, slight aging).
- **Borders:** Hand-drawn vine/knot SVG borders in oxblood (#8a2418) or dark ink (#2b2420).
- **Illuminated initial:** Gold-and-red drop cap on first grapheme (SVG or CSS gradient).
- **Headings:** Rubricated (red ink #b83b3b) for section heads.
- **Wax seal:** Small SVG wax seal icon for authenticity mark.
- **Candle vignette:** Subtle radial gradient / vignette to suggest candlelight.
- **Easter eggs (original art only):**
  - Optional: Kutná Hora ossuary skull (tiny, decorative, toggleable).
  - Optional: Prague golem with "emet" (single stroke, subtle watermark).
  - No Kingdom Come: Deliverance IP.
